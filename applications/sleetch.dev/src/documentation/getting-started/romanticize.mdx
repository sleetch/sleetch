---

Yes, romanticize it.  
Write it like a love letter.  
Think about the future.  
Think about the people who will take the time to read it.

Writing code is an art form in itself, but writing its documentation is a way to share and explain all the *secrets, rules, and chaos* that hide in that art.

Documentation shouldn't just be readable.  
It should spark something in a developer's mind and make their eyes light up when they read it.  
Isn't that what matters most ?   
The best way to leave a mark with your code ?

Now, **romanticize your documentation** without procrastinating, not later, not in five minutes, not tomorrow.  
Don't leave this task to LLMs.  
Just do it.  
It's easy.

Leave your human mark everywhere, whatever you do.  
For love, peace, and a better world <3

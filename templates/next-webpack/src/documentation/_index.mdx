# Iris Flowers: Nature's Rainbow

> **"The iris is the flower of the gods, a bridge between heaven and earth."**
> — Ancient Greek Proverb

![Iris Rainbow](/iris-rainbow.jpg "A collection of iris flowers showing their diverse colors")

The *Iris* genus, named after the Greek goddess of the rainbow, encompasses over **300 species** of flowering plants with stunning diversity in color, form, and habitat. This documentation explores every aspect of irises - from botanical classification to cultural significance, cultivation techniques to scientific research.

## Why Irises?

Irises are more than just beautiful flowers:

- **🎨 Color Diversity**: From deepest purples to brightest yellows, with intricate patterns
- **🌍 Adaptability**: Species for every garden condition - wet, dry, sun, shade
- **📜 Rich History**: Cultivated for thousands of years across multiple civilizations
- **🧪 Scientific Value**: Important in genetics, medicine, and ecology
- **🐝 Pollinator Friendly**: Vital food source for bees and other pollinators

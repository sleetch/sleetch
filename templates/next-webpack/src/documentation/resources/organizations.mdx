# Organizations & Societies

> **"Alone we can grow irises, but together we can cultivate a world of beauty and knowledge."**

Joining an **iris society** is one of the **best ways** to **learn, connect, and grow** as an iris enthusiast. These organizations offer **expert advice, shows, sales, research, and community** to help you get the most out of your iris passion.

## 🌍 International Organizations

## 💡 How to Get Involved

### **Steps to Join an Iris Society:**

```md
**Step 1: Find Your Local Society**
- Check the **American Iris Society** website for regional societies
- Search for **iris societies in your country**
- Ask at **local nurseries or botanical gardens**

**Step 2: Attend a Meeting or Event**
- Visit a **local iris show**
- Attend a **garden tour**
- Participate in a **workshop or seminar**

**Step 3: Join the Society**
- Fill out a **membership application**
- Pay the **annual dues**
- Receive your **membership benefits**

**Step 4: Get Involved**
- Volunteer at **shows and events**
- Participate in **rhizome or seed exchanges**
- Join a **committee or special interest group**
- Enter your **irises in shows**
- Share your **knowledge and experience**
```

### **Benefits of Joining an Iris Society:**

✅ **Access to Expert Knowledge**
- Learn from **experienced iris growers**
- Get **personalized advice** for your garden
- Access **cultivation guides and resources**

✅ **Networking Opportunities**
- Connect with **fellow iris enthusiasts**
- Share **tips, tricks, and experiences**
- Build **friendships and community**

✅ **Educational Resources**
- Attend **workshops, seminars, and lectures**
- Access **newsletters, journals, and books**
- Learn about **new iris varieties and trends**

✅ **Shows and Events**
- Participate in **iris shows and competitions**
- Visit **garden tours and open days**
- Attend **conventions and conferences**

✅ **Plant Exchange Programs**
- Participate in **rhizome and seed exchanges**
- Access **rare and unusual iris varieties**
- Share your **own iris divisions and seeds**

✅ **Awards and Recognition**
- Enter your **irises in competitions**
- Receive **recognition for outstanding irises**
- Earn **awards for hybridizing and cultivation**

✅ **Support for Research and Conservation**
- Support **iris research and breeding**
- Participate in **conservation efforts**
- Help **preserve historic iris cultivars**

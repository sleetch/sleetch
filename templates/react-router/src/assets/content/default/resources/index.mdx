## Featured Resources

### 🌟 Top 5 Must-Have Resources

#### 1. **American Iris Society (AIS)**
- **Type**: Organization
- **Website**: [www.irises.org](https://www.irises.org)
- **Why It's Essential**: The **premier organization** for iris enthusiasts, offering:
  - Official **iris registry**
  - **Hybridizing** information
  - **Shows and events**
  - **Publications and newsletters**
  - **Regional societies**
  - **Expert advice**

#### 2. **The Genus Iris by William R. Dykes**
- **Type**: Book
- **First Published**: 1913
- **Why It's Essential**: The **definitive reference** on iris classification and taxonomy:
  - **Comprehensive species descriptions**
  - **Historical context**
  - **Classification system** still used today
  - **Beautiful illustrations**

#### 3. **Schreiner's Iris Gardens**
- **Type**: Nursery
- **Location**: Salem, Oregon, USA
- **Website**: [www.schreinersgardens.com](https://www.schreinersgardens.com)
- **Why It's Essential**: One of the **largest and most reputable** iris nurseries:
  - **Huge selection** of bearded irises
  - **High-quality rhizomes**
  - **Expert advice**
  - **Mail order** available

#### 4. **Iris Wiki**
- **Type**: Online Encyclopedia
- **Website**: [wiki.irises.org](https://wiki.irises.org)
- **Why It's Essential**: A **collaborative, comprehensive** resource:
  - **Species and cultivar information**
  - **Cultivation guides**
  - **Hybridizing information**
  - **Pest and disease management**
  - **Historical information**

#### 5. **The World of Irises by Brian Mathew**
- **Type**: Book
- **Why It's Essential**: The **most comprehensive modern guide** to irises:
  - **All iris species** covered
  - **Beautiful photography**
  - **Cultivation advice**
  - **Historical context**

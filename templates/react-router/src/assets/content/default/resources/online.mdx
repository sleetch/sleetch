### 🔍 Identification & Classification

#### **Iris Register (AIS)**

```
Iris Register Profile:
├── **URL**: https://www.irises.org/register
├── **Maintained by**: American Iris Society
├── **Content**: Official database of registered iris cultivars
├── **Coverage**: 50,000+ registered cultivars
├── **Searchable**: Yes (by name, color, year, hybridizer)
├── **Images**: Some entries include photos
├── **Updates**: Regularly updated with new registrations
└── **Access**: Free to search, registration required for submissions
```

**Features:**
- **Advanced search** by multiple criteria
- **Cultivar details** (parentage, hybridizer, year, color)
- **Awards information** (Dykes Medal winners, etc.)
- **Historical information** on older cultivars
- **Hybridizer information** and statistics

**How to Use:**
1. Visit [irises.org/register](https://www.irises.org/register)
2. Use the **search function** to find specific cultivars
3. Browse by **category** (bearded, beardless, bulbous, etc.)
4. Check **award winners** for top-performing cultivars
5. View **hybridizer information** to learn about breeders

## 💡 Tips for Using Online Resources

### **Getting the Most Out of Online Resources**

```md
✅ **DO**:
- **Verify information** from multiple sources
- **Check dates** to ensure information is current
- **Read reviews** before purchasing from online nurseries
- **Participate in forums** to learn from others
- **Contribute your knowledge** to help the community
- **Use multiple resources** for comprehensive information
- **Bookmark favorite sites** for easy access
- **Sign up for newsletters** to stay updated

❌ **DON'T**:
- **Trust unverified information** without checking
- **Buy from unreliable sellers** without research
- **Post without checking** facts first
- **Ignore community guidelines** in forums
- **Share personal information** publicly
- **Download copyrighted material** illegally
- **Spam forums** with off-topic posts
```

### **Evaluating Online Information**

**Check for:**
- **Author credentials** (expertise, experience)
- **Date of publication** (currency)
- **Sources cited** (reliability)
- **Consistency** with other sources
- **Bias or conflicts of interest**
- **User reviews and ratings**
